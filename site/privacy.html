Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: FamiDuel has no accounts, no chat and no tracking. Your names and scores never leave your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between matches; those ads are set to be family-safe and never personalized, and you can remove them with one purchase.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things in its own storage on your phone: the player names you type, your settings, scores, match-night and tournament progress, your best score on the daily minigolf course, and whether you bought Remove Ads. This information never leaves your device, and neither we nor anyone else can see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep every game free, the app shows a full-screen ad between matches now and then. The ads come from Google AdMob. We set every ad request to be treated as directed to children and under the age of consent, to show only ads rated for general audiences, and to be non-personalized. This means ads are chosen from what is on screen and general information such as the rough region, not from a profile of you.

To show and count ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software receives some information from the phone, such as its IP address (from which a rough location can be estimated), the device model and system version, an app-specific device identifier, which ads were shown and tapped, and crash and performance data. The app does not ask to track you, so it cannot read the phone's advertising identifier. We do not receive or see this information. Google's use of it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple ID owns Remove Ads. The purchase sits behind a grown-ups question, so a child can't buy it by accident.

Children

FamiDuel is made for families, including young children. We collect no personal information from anyone, children included: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to share. The ad settings above follow the rules for apps that children may use (such as COPPA in the US and the GDPR in Europe).

Your choices

Buy Remove Ads to stop all ads and the information Google receives for them. Turning off the internet also stops ads. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
