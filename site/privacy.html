Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

FamiDuel does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no chat, and it never sends anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves a few things in its own storage on your phone: the player names you type, your settings, scores, match-night and tournament progress, and your best score on the daily minigolf course. This information never leaves your device, and neither we nor anyone else can see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

FamiDuel is made for families, including young children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There is nothing for a child to sign up for, share or buy.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and shares no information with anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this (for example ads or an in-app purchase), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
