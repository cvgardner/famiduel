FamiDuel

Two players. One phone. Super powers.

FamiDuel is a collection of 1v1 games for two people and one phone: friends, couples, roommates or a parent and a kid. Lay the phone flat on the table between you; each player owns the half nearest them. Twelve one-finger games, from Air Hockey and Minigolf to Sumo and Penalty Shootout, each with super powers to pick, an honest bot to play alone, and a handicap that keeps matches close, whoever you play.

Free to play. A short family-safe ad can show between matches (never during one), and one purchase removes them. No accounts, no chat, no tracking.
