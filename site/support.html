Common questions

How do two people play on one phone? Lay the phone flat between you. The bottom player plays the bottom half, the top player the top half, and the top player's words are turned to face them.

Can I play alone? Yes. Tap "Play the bot" on the home screen. Each game's bot plays by the same rules you do, from easy to hard.

How do I pause? Press and hold the pause button in the middle of the screen until its ring fills. A quick tap won't pause, so a stray tap never stops the game.

How do I turn on super powers? Settings, then Super powers. Each player then picks one of three powers before a match.

Can I stop my child leaving the game? Yes, with the iPhone's own Guided Access: Settings, then "Grown-ups corner", shows how.

How do I remove the ads? Settings, then "For grown-ups", then Remove ads. It's one purchase, answered behind a grown-ups question. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchase there (same Apple ID), and it's free again.

When do ads show? Only between matches, at most one every couple of matches and a few minutes apart, never during a game and never the first time you open the app.

Where are our names and scores kept? Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.
